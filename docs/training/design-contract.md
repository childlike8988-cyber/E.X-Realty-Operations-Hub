# AI Realty Training Center — Design Contract

This contract records the future product direction for `E.X Realty Operations Hub/training`. Stage 1 only supplies domain foundations and a deliberately minimal catalog route; it does not implement the visual system below.

## Product craft

- **Clarity first:** one obvious task and one clear next action at a time.
- **Typography hierarchy:** scenario name, persona, skill focus, difficulty, and status should scan in that order.
- **Generous whitespace:** use space to separate briefing, conversation, evidence, and coaching rather than adding more cards.
- **Progressive disclosure:** reveal hidden scenario information through interaction; do not expose every rule in the first frame.
- **Direct manipulation:** conversation and evidence actions should respond directly to pointer, touch, and keyboard input.
- **Immediate feedback:** acknowledge an action quickly with a restrained state change or status update.
- **Interruptible motion:** transitions must yield to a new action and never block navigation.

## Material hierarchy

The future UI should use a light Realty Operations Hub surface, solid primary content surfaces, light glass for floating controls, medium glass for an inspector or modal, and high-contrast solid treatment for critical risk information. Avoid stacking glass on glass and avoid making every item glow.

## Accessibility

Support `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-transparency`, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and controls, adequate contrast, and touch targets of at least 44px. Reduced motion keeps information hierarchy and removes non-essential movement.

This is an E.X product direction, not an Apple-branded interface or clone.
